How it works

Mine, pulse, get paid.

Pulse is a lamp-lit cave you mine with timed strikes. While you mine, your phone quietly proves it is online and doing its part.

1

Strike the vein

Tap when the marker hits the gold zone. Perfect strikes break veins faster and drop more ore for the forge.

2

Your phone pulses

Every 30 seconds it signs a heartbeat with its own key. The key never leaves the phone.

3

Paid every 10 minutes

Each accepted heartbeat is one unit. Ten units make 1 PLS, settled into your balance every ten minutes.

Stake your PLS

Two halls. Skill or luck.

Gear helps you mine, never in a duel. Skill games give both players the exact same challenge. Luck games are provably fair, so you can check every roll yourself.

The Arena

Skill wagering
  • QuickdrawFive rounds. Tap the moment the pad turns green.
  • Rune SequenceWatch the runes light up and repeat them, one longer each round.
  • Vein SniperTwelve veins flash on a grid. Faster hits score more.

Ranked 1v1 duels and weekly cups. 5% of every duel pot is burned.

The Gambit Hall

Luck wagering
  • Crystal FlipCall teal or amber. Pays 1.94x.
  • Ore DiceRoll 0.00 to 99.99 and land under your target.
  • Cave-InUncover safe tiles on a 5x5 field and cash out before the roof falls.

3% house edge, about 97% paid back. Every result comes from a seed you can verify.

PULSE on Solana

Proof you can check.

Pulse runs on its own servers for the fast parts: handshakes, heartbeats and rewards. Solana is where ownership lives, so anyone can see how much PULSE exists and where it went.

Right now that runs on Solana devnet, a public test network. Every node reward is also minted as devnet PULSE to the player's own wallet, and nothing else mints it.

Devnet PULSE has no cash value. Your in-game PLS stays separate from it.

Built to resist cheating

A node has to earn its place.

Signed by the phone

Every handshake and heartbeat carries a signature only that phone's key can make.

No replays

Each heartbeat has a number that only goes up. An old one is refused.

Real mining only

Heartbeats count only when the server sees genuine mining strikes from your account.

One phone earns at a time

Extra phones on one account sit out while another is earning.

Caps on issuance

A daily limit per player and an hourly limit for the whole network keep supply in check.

Paid exactly once

Every payout has a record, and the ledger always balances to zero.

The future

Deeper levels ahead.

Pulse goes down one level at a time. Nothing moves to Solana mainnet before the token plan is final and the legal review is done.

Level 1

Phone nodes Live

Registration, signed handshakes and heartbeats while you mine.

Level 2

Reward ledger Live

Node time becomes PLS every ten minutes, with full records and audits.

Level 3

Devnet PULSE Live

Node rewards minted as a test token on Solana, visible to anyone.

Level 4

Real-phone testing Now

A trial with real players to tune the reward rate and catch abuse.

Level 5

Final token plan Next

A fixed maximum supply, released to nodes on a set schedule.

Level 6

Wallet connect Later

Connect Phantom or Solflare straight from the app on Android.

Level 7

Mainnet, after legal review Later

PULSE on Solana mainnet, with authorities held by a multisig and every permission documented.

Pulse Network is in testing. PLS and devnet PULSE are test balances with no cash value, cannot be bought, and are not an investment.
