    For the nerds: how a check-in works
    • Each phone makes its own RSA-2048 key pair on first launch. The private key never leaves the phone.
    • The phone opens a session with a signed handshake, then sends a signed heartbeat every 30 seconds.
    • Every heartbeat carries a sequence number that must keep going up, so recorded heartbeats can't be replayed.
    • Settlement runs every 600 seconds and pays each node exactly once per period.

    Stake Your PLS
    Back yourself.

    Put your PLS on the line against real players. There are two halls, and each takes a different kind of nerve.

    The Arena PURE SKILL

    Both players get the exact same challenge. Same pattern, same timing, same everything. The only difference is you.

    Quickdraw: Five rounds. Tap the instant the pad flashes green. Fastest hands win.
    Rune Sequence: Memorize the glowing runes. Each round adds one more. Who cracks first?
    Vein Sniper: Twelve veins flash across a grid. Hit them fast. Hit them first.
    The Gambit Hall PURE LUCK

    Every roll is sealed before you play. Afterward you can unseal it and prove nobody cheated, not even us.

    Crystal Flip: Teal or amber. Call it.
    Ore Dice: Pick your target and roll under it.
    Cave-In: Dig safe tiles and cash out before the roof comes down.
    🔥 5% of every duel pot is burned. It's gone forever, so every duel makes PLS a little rarer.
    PULSE on Solana
    Every coin, out in the open.

    PULSE lives on Solana, one of the fastest blockchains on the planet. A blockchain is a public record that nobody can secretly edit, so anyone, anywhere can count every PULSE that exists and see exactly where it went.

    Link a Solana wallet in the app and every reward you earn as a node is also sent to you as a real PULSE token, in your own wallet, under your own control.

    Right now this runs on Solana's test network, the full dress rehearsal before launch. Test tokens, real tech.

    Fair Play
    Cheaters don't get paid.

    A network that pays people is a network people will try to fool. Pulse was built for that from day one.

    🖐
    Fingerprinted phonesEach phone has a secret key that never leaves it. A faked check-in won't match.
    🔁
    No copy-pasteEvery check-in is numbered. Send an old one again and it bounces.
    ⛏
    Real swings onlyCheck-ins only count while you're actually mining. Idle phones don't earn.
    📱
    One phone, one paycheckTen phones on one account don't earn ten times. Only one earns at a time.
    🚦
    Speed limits on new coinsDaily limits per player and a cap for the whole network keep supply in check.
    📒
    Books that always balanceEvery coin is recorded leaving one place and arriving in another. Nothing appears from thin air.
