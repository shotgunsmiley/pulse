Check in

While you mine, your phone becomes a node, a working part of the network. It checks in every 30 seconds, signed with a digital fingerprint only your phone can make.

💎

Payday

Every 10 minutes the network adds up every check-in and pays out. Ten check-ins earn you 1 PLS, the Pulse coin.

    For the nerds: how a check-in works
    • Each phone makes its own RSA-2048 key pair on first launch. The private key never leaves the phone.
    • The phone opens a session with a signed handshake, then sends a signed heartbeat every 30 seconds.
    • Every heartbeat carries a sequence number that must keep going up, so recorded heartbeats can't be replayed.
    • Settlement runs every 600 seconds and pays each node exactly once per period.

    PULSE on Solana

    Every coin,
    out in the open.

    PULSE lives on Solana, one of the fastest blockchains on the planet. A blockchain is a public record that nobody can secretly edit. So anyone, anywhere can count every PULSE that exists and see exactly where it went.

    Link a Solana wallet in the app and every reward you earn as a node is also sent to you as a real PULSE token, in your own wallet, under your own control.

    Fair play

    Cheaters don't get paid.

    A network that pays people is a network people will try to fool. Pulse was built for that from day one.

    🖐

    Fingerprinted phones

    Each phone has a secret key that never leaves it. A faked check-in won't match.

    🔁

    No copy-paste

    Every check-in is numbered. Send an old one again and it bounces.

    ⛏

    Real swings only

    Check-ins only count while you're actually mining. Idle phones don't earn.

    📱

    One phone, one paycheck

    Ten phones on one account don't earn ten times. Only one earns at a time.

    🚦

    Speed limits on new coins

    Daily limits per player and a cap for the whole network keep supply in check.

    📒

    Books that always balance

    Every coin is recorded leaving one place and arriving in another. Nothing appears from thin air.
